# Hardware and building synchronization audit

## Scope and evidence rules

This audit covers the physical-PC/building side of Free Build: saved parts and installations, maintenance and breakage, cable and water-cooling graphs, case appearance, lighting/fans, peripherals, and physical power/boot effects. It uses local source artifacts only; no running-game observation was made. The `AGENTS.md` findings are context, not new runtime verification.

**Current mod status:** all factors below are **Missing**. The only network payload found is `PlayerState(X,Y,Z,Yaw,InWorkshop)` in [Protocol.cs](../../src/PCBS2Coop.Network/Protocol.cs#L14). No PC/building state appears in the protocol or plugin. Candidate owner and kind labels are investigation aids, not design decisions.

**Evidence levels:** **Declaration** = generated C# field/property/method signature; dumped C# bodies are placeholders and prove no behavior. **Native/ISIL** = native disassembly/ISIL body or method label; stronger method evidence but not a game-run observation. **Inference** = interpretation from names, type shape, or method signatures. Mentioned state can have overlapping save/model/Unity representations. `Derived` means likely reconstructed locally; `local-pending` means transient gesture or asset/scene dependency whose sync boundary is unresolved.

## Identity, concurrency, and restoration risks

- A part SKU is not demonstrated to be a unique instance key. Slot index/order, scene path, and connector ID are candidates, but stability across case replacement, load, DLC and rebuild is unverified; repeated same-SKU parts make SKU-keyed edits ambiguous.
- Simultaneous install/remove, screw, latch, cable endpoint/route, pipe, coolant, paint, or sticker edits can invalidate each other's assumptions. No authority, conflict resolution, operation ID, rollback, lock, or idempotency scheme is present in the evidence.
- Scene restoration may receive state before case, slots, component prefabs, connector caches, and theme/sticker assets exist. Installation APIs depend on loaded assets and camera/slot scene objects; late join/re-entry order needs proof.
- Collision, blockers, radiator/loop validity, power, fan rotation, LED output, air pressure, thermals, and customization coverage may be locally derived. Replicating both inputs and outcomes risks divergence/feedback loops across versions or assets.
- Custom theme/sticker textures, part/case prefabs, and DLC content may be unavailable or versioned differently. The protocol has no hardware asset manifest/checksum.
- Installing, tightening screws, moving latches, brushing paste/dust, routing cables/pipes, painting/stickering, and plugging peripherals may have transient animation/gesture state. Whether remote intent should be shown or only committed outcomes synchronized is an owner decision.
- `PartInstance` runtime component references coexist with saved logical fields; prevent echoing local visual recalculation as a fresh remote mutation.
