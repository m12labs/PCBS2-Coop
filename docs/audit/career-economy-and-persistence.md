# Career, economy, and persistence sync audit

Scope: career/economy/mode/persistence surfaces in the local decompile and the current Coop plugin/network source. This is a source audit, not a running-game verification. Decompiled C# is treated as declarations/serialized shape where method bodies are `{ }`; behavior statements below are based on native ISIL or are explicitly marked inference. Current network sync is **Missing** for every row below: the inspected protocol carries only `PlayerState` (XYZ, yaw, workshop flag), and the plugin consumes it to render a remote capsule. No career, world, economy, save, or authority messages are present in the inspected network/plugin files.

The owner has not chosen shared-wallet, shared-inventory, shared-progression, or shared-save semantics. Rows therefore identify candidate authority/state boundaries and the decisions still open; they do not imply a design choice. Root audit covers player/workshop and session, while hardware and software/screens are covered by the sibling audits.
